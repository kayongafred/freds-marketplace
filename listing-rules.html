Listing Rules

Effective date: October 30, 2026

These rules help keep Kayonga eMarket useful and safer for buyers and sellers. They apply to every product listing and are part of our Terms of Use.

Every listing must

  • Offer a real item that you are authorized to sell and can make available to a buyer.
  • Use an accurate title, category, description, and condition. Any asking price shown with a listing must be accurate; buyers and sellers should confirm price and terms directly. Do not misrepresent what is included or hide important defects.
  • Use a clear photo of the actual item or an image you have permission to use.
  • Include contact details you are authorized to share publicly. Your seller name, email, phone, location, and photo are visible to visitors.
  • Be courteous and communicate honestly with buyers.

Do not list

  • Stolen, counterfeit, pirated, recalled, or otherwise unlawful goods.
  • Weapons, ammunition, explosives, controlled drugs, prescription medicines, or other dangerous or regulated products.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, or products that cannot lawfully be sold to the intended buyer.
  • Items you do not possess or cannot deliver, fake offers, bait-and-switch offers, or misleading claims.
  • Content that violates someone’s privacy, intellectual-property rights, or safety, or that is abusive, hateful, sexually exploitative, or otherwise unlawful.
  • Spam, duplicate listings intended to manipulate search, requests for passwords or verification codes, or links designed to mislead or defraud.

Report a listing

Buyers should inspect items and independently agree on payment, delivery, or pickup with the seller. Kayonga eMarket does not handle payments or guarantee transactions. To report a listing or request review or removal, email kayongafred87@gmail.com and include the listing link and reason. We may remove content that appears to break these rules.

These rules do not replace applicable laws. Sellers are responsible for checking whether an item may be sold where they are and where the buyer is.
