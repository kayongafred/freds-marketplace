Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Kayonga eMarket handles information when you browse the marketplace or submit a listing. The website is a marketplace listing service; buyers and sellers arrange transactions directly with one another.

Information we handle

  • Listing details: product name, category, description, photo, seller name, email, phone number, and the address or area you provide. The current posting form does not ask for an asking price; if a price is present in a listing record, it may be displayed with that listing.
  • Technical information: when the shared database is enabled, Supabase creates an anonymous seller session so a listing can be submitted. Hosting, database, security, and spam-prevention providers may process network, device, and request information needed to deliver and protect the service.
  • Browser storage: if the shared database is not configured or available, listings created in that browser may be stored in your browser’s local storage on that device.

What is public

When you publish a listing, its product details, photo, seller name, email, phone number, and address or area are made publicly viewable so potential buyers can contact you. An asking price is shown only when one is present in the listing record. Search engines, other visitors, and third parties may copy, cache, or share public information. Do not submit a home address or contact detail unless you are comfortable making it public. You may instead provide a less precise pickup area.

How information is used

We use listing information to display and operate the marketplace, let buyers contact sellers, prevent spam and misuse, maintain the service, and respond to support or removal requests. If optional new-listing email notifications are configured, listing details may also be sent to the marketplace administrator by email.

Service providers and sharing

The website uses or may use Netlify for hosting, Supabase for anonymous sessions, database and photo storage, and hCaptcha for spam prevention. It loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell listing information. We may disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

Retention and your choices

Public listings remain available until removed. To ask a question or request that a listing be reviewed or removed, email kayongafred87@gmail.com and include the listing details or link. We will review the request and take reasonable steps to address it. Copies may remain in backups, browser storage, search-engine caches, or third-party shares for some time. You can clear this website’s local storage in your browser settings.

Security and children

We use the protections provided by the website’s hosting and database configuration, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send sensitive identity, financial, or account credentials through a listing. The marketplace is not designed for children to submit personal information; a parent or guardian should contact us if a child has submitted information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Questions or requests can be sent to kayongafred87@gmail.com.

This policy is a general description of the current website and is not legal advice. Privacy rights and requirements may vary depending on where you live.
